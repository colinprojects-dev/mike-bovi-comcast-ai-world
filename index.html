Distinguish the products and techniques

FDI is discussed through its inspected analytics, semantic-layer and extension sessions. The earlier dataset’s Fusion ERP Analytics label does not establish equivalence, entitlement or migration to FDI. OTBI and Oracle Analytics are distinct products and experiences; this guide makes no automatic interoperability claim. Embedded Fusion AI, Agent Studio, Oracle Integration and supporting OCI services also have different scopes.

Choosing a model, connecting an external endpoint, retrieving enterprise data (including RAG), customizing a model and hosting inference are separate decisions. A customer-selected model does not by itself resolve data handling, security or contractual concerns. Ask the relevant Oracle application, analytics, integration, security and legal specialists for the exact supported design.

Interpretation and comparison limits

Fit, rank, topic tags, comparison basis and the provisional agenda are planning judgments. Exact titles, abstracts, IDs, published times, rooms, formats and speaker affiliations come from the inspected catalog evidence. Missing fields say “Not provided”, which does not imply no fee, equipment or registration requirement. Product roadmaps, demonstrations, customer results, preparation and concepts are distinguished. A roadmap is not a release commitment.

Customer comparisons identify the disclosed workflow, product scope or adoption practice. Similar brand size does not establish matching scale, maturity, modules or time on Fusion. HCM-origin enablement and release examples are labeled as transferable methods, not AP-specific results. The three-, six- and twelve-month horizons organize questions, without assigning features or delivery dates. The crawl, walk, run framework is not an assessment of Comcast.
