Distinguish the products and techniques

FDI is discussed through its inspected analytics, semantic-layer and extension sessions. The earlier dataset’s Fusion ERP Analytics label does not establish equivalence, entitlement or migration to FDI. OTBI and Oracle Analytics are distinct products and experiences; this guide makes no automatic interoperability claim. Embedded Fusion AI, Agent Studio, Oracle Integration and supporting OCI services also have different scopes.

Choosing a model, connecting an external endpoint, retrieving enterprise data (including RAG), customizing a model and hosting inference are separate decisions. A customer-selected model does not by itself resolve data handling, security or contractual concerns. Ask the relevant Oracle application, analytics, integration, security and legal specialists for the exact supported design.

Interpretation is separate from catalog facts

Fit, functional relevance, provisional rank and the “why it matters” explanations are planning judgments. Exact titles, IDs, offerings, rooms, formats and speaker affiliations come from inspected catalog details. Missing fields read “Not provided”; that does not mean no fee or requirement. General seats are first-come, first-served; adding to this plan does not reserve a seat. Roadmaps and demonstrations are distinct from customer results.
